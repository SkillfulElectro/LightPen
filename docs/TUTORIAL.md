# Using LightPen (non-technical guide)

## 1. Open a PDF

Click **Open PDF** in the toolbar, or drag a PDF onto the window. The built-in
**Practice PDF** button loads a sample you can scribble on.

## 2. Turn on the camera

Click **Camera** in the toolbar. A short note explains what the camera is for:
your video is processed on this device and never uploaded. Nothing is recorded.

Click **Allow camera**. If the operating system asks for permission, accept it.
If you say no, that is fine: you can draw with a mouse, trackpad, finger or stylus.

## 3. Place the camera

- Put the camera at roughly eye level, an arm's length away.
- Make sure your hand and forearm fit in the picture.
- Light your hand from the front; avoid a bright window behind you.
- Keep your hand about 40–70 cm from the camera.

## 4. Calibrate

The tutorial asks you to drag a box over the area in which you will move your
hand. Everything inside that box becomes the page. Draw the box comfortably: you
do not have to reach the corners of the camera frame.

You can redo this any time with **Settings → Calibration** (reset, then re-run the
tutorial from **Help → Tutorial**).

## 5. Practice

Hover your index finger; a blue dot follows it. Pinch thumb and index together
to draw (the dot turns red). Open your palm over a line to erase it. Swipe
sideways with an open palm to change pages, and hold a fist for one second to
pause.

The **Gesture cheat sheet** button (✋) shows the same list.

## 6. Save

**Save annotated PDF** writes a new file — your original PDF is untouched.
Annotations are also saved automatically next to the app data as
`<name>.lightpen.json` and reappear when you open the same PDF again.
